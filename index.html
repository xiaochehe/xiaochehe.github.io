Cunling Bian / README.md

Understanding classrooms through AI

I am a lecturer at the Center for Fundamental Education, Ocean University of China. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Tianjin University in 2023. My research focuses on multimodal classroom analysis, learning behavior modeling, and generative AI in education. I work with authentic classroom data, computational methods, and empirical educational research to understand teaching and learning.

Research interests

CLASSROOM INTELLIGENCE

Multimodal analysis & behavior modeling

Classroom datasets, teacher–student behavior recognition, classroom climate and group engagement, and generative agents for learning behavior simulation.

GENERATIVE AI IN EDUCATION

Human–AI learning & educational applications

Learning effects of AI-generated content, human–AI collaboration, and critical engagement with generative AI in education.
